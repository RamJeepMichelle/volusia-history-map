# Historic Volusia County Sightseeing Map

**21 historic stops in and around Volusia County, Florida, on one free interactive map.** Lighthouses, sugar mill ruins, Timucuan shell mounds, speed history and civil rights landmarks from Ormond Beach to Canaveral National Seashore and west to DeLand. Filter by theme, tap a pin for the story, and get directions.

**Open the map:** https://ramjeepmichelle.github.io/volusia-history-map/

Built by **Jeep Michelle**, a Volusia local history buff, Halifax Historical Society volunteer, and Jeep and Ram commercial fleet specialist at New Smyrna Chrysler Jeep Dodge Ram in New Smyrna Beach.

## The stops

| Theme | Sites |
| --- | --- |
| Timucua & colonial | Tomoka State Park, Old Fort Park (Turnbull Ruins), New Smyrna Museum of History, Turtle Mound |
| Plantations & ruins | Dunlawton Sugar Mill Gardens, New Smyrna Sugar Mill Ruins, Bulow Plantation Ruins (Flagler County) |
| Speed & racing | Streamline Hotel, Daytona International Speedway, Birthplace of Speed Park |
| Civil rights & education | Mary McLeod Bethune Home & Bethune-Cookman University, Jackie Robinson Ballpark, Stetson University |
| Museums & towns | Halifax Historical Museum, Ponce de Leon Inlet Lighthouse, The Casements, Stetson Mansion, All Saints Episcopal Church (Enterprise) |
| Springs & old Florida | De Leon Springs State Park, Blue Spring State Park, Cassadaga Spiritualist Camp |

Site details live in `sites.json`, so adding a stop is one entry. Coordinates come from OpenStreetMap. Hours, fees and access change, so check each site before you go.
